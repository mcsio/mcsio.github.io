LineAlert for iPhone

Above the line, or below it.

LineAlert watches your tickers and tells you the day the 8-day EMA crosses the 20-day EMA. One list, one arrow per stock, one push when it flips.

Coming to the App Store   Questions

What it does

▲ Above the line

Fast EMA is above the slow EMA. Trend is with you.

▼ Below the line

Fast EMA is below the slow EMA. Trend is against you.

Same-day alerts

Pro checks every close at 4:05pm ET and pushes the flip to your phone, app closed or not.

Chart with every cross

Six months of closes with both lines and a marker at each cross, so you can see how the line has treated that stock.

What it doesn't do

No accounts. No news. No trading. No intraday noise: the line is computed on daily closes only, so one alert per real change, not twenty per afternoon.

Pricing

Free: up to 3 tickers, alerts when the app refreshes in the background.
Pro, $3.99/month or $29.99/year: unlimited tickers and same-day push alerts after the close.

Your settings

Periods are adjustable (default 8 and 20). Bring your own Twelve Data key if you want a licensed feed on the free tier; it stays in your iPhone's Keychain.

LineAlert is a math tool, not investment advice. Prices are end-of-day and may be delayed or wrong.

Next: a rules companion for Schwab accounts

LineAlert watches one line. I'm thinking about a bigger tool for people who trade by their own written rules on Schwab: you write the rules, it connects to your own Schwab account and checks every one of them after each close.

  • Rule checks: "exit on a close below my line," "no position over X% of the book," "keep Y% in cash." You get one message when a rule is broken.
  • Risk across all your accounts at once: beta-weighted exposure and how much of it your hedge covers.
  • A journal that holds you to it: what you said you'd do, next to what you did.

It doesn't pick stocks or trade for you. You'd connect it with your own Schwab API key. I haven't built it, and I'll only build it if enough people want it.
